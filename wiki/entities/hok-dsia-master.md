The master's programme at HOK | Hochschule für Angewandte Wissenschaften Kufstein Tirol in which the thesis is written; the author attends the part-time (bb) form.

## Facts
- MSc (Master of Science in Engineering), 4 semesters, 120 ECTS; full-time and part-time ([[dsia-studienordnung-2027]]).
- Part-time: taught in German with about 35 % English courses; 25 entry places per year ([[dsia-studienordnung-2027]]).
- Modules: SDDE (software & data engineering), MLAL (ML & algorithms), MDS (management), DPR (data processing), WPF (electives), MWA (thesis & scientific work), ETHR (ethics & law) ([[dsia-studienordnung-2027]]).
- Thesis: 22 ECTS in semester 4 (20 for the thesis, 2 for the committee exam); exposé approved by the programme director; see [[thesis-timeline]] ([[dsia-studienordnung-2027]]).
- Tuition EUR 363.36 per semester, from an older third-party listing; not in the regulation ([[hok-dsia-program-listings]]).
- Thesis topic: OSM-object recognition and classification in orthoimagery, with a PoC platform ([[thesis-goal]]).

## Courses that feed the thesis
MLAL.3 ML & Deep Learning (CNNs), MLAL.1/.5 Statistical Learning (baselines), MDS.6 DS for
Engineering & Natural Sciences (object recognition from images), DPR.9 AI (visual object
recognition), MDS.4 Praxisprojekt, WPF.1 analytics/cloud platforms, WPF.8 Human-AI
Interaction, ETHR.1 law (GDPR, AI Act) ([[dsia-studienordnung-2027]]).
