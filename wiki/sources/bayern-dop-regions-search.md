Search-snippet facts: Bavarian 20 cm orthophotos (DOP20, RGB and colour-infrared) are open data under CC BY 4.0; southern Upper Bavaria corresponds to planning regions Oberland (3,956 km²) and Südostoberbayern (5,225 km²).

## Summary
The Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung publishes DOP20 (colour, grey, colour-infrared) under CC BY 4.0 with
WMS access. An OSM community note says CC BY 4.0 is ODbL-incompatible for *mapping into* OSM. No official
"Oberbayern Süd" exists; the southern planning regions are 17 Oberland and 18 Südostoberbayern.

## Key claims
- **DOP20:** 20 cm, RGB + CIR, CC BY 4.0, attribution "Datenquelle: Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung – www.geodaten.bayern.de"; WMS available. The bulk-download path is unconfirmed.
- Using it for OSM edits is disputed (licence clause vs. ODbL), per a May 2026 OSM talk page.
- Region 17 Oberland: 3,956 km² (TÖL, GAP, MB, WM). Region 18 Südostoberbayern: 5,225 km², 159 residents/km² (2017).

## Relevance to this wiki
- **DOP20 is training and test imagery inside the search region,** at the thesis resolution, openly licensed ([[imagery-sources]]).
- The CIR band gives NIR, which helps vegetation separation ([[spectral-texture-features]]).
- The thesis reads OSM; it does not write back to OSM, so the ODbL issue only matters if results were contributed to OSM.
