Scope decision by the author: the search region (geolocation prior and study area) is southern Upper Bavaria ("Oberbayern Süd").

## Summary
Sets the region prior needed by metadata-free geolocation ([[author-decision-no-metadata]]). It is
operationalised in [[oberbayern-sued]].

## Key claims
- The search region is Oberbayern Süd.
- Follow-up: the selected definition (Oberland + Südostoberbayern) is used **for now**; a different area may be chosen later, so the region is **provisional**.
- Follow-up 2: **only the Alpine-edge districts for now**: GAP, TÖL, MB, WM, RO (+ Rosenheim city), TS, BGL. Altötting and Mühldorf are dropped.

## Relevance to this wiki
Fixes the region for the OSM index, the imagery and the uniqueness estimates in [[osm-fingerprint-geolocalization]].
It replaces the earlier Tyrol examples, though the region borders Tyrol and Kufstein (HOK).
