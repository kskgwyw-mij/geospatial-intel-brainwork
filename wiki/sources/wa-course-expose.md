Summary notes of the two slide decks for HOK's scientific-writing course in WS 2026/27. They contain the exposé assignment (structure, deadline, name-free PDF, AI documentation with a 50 % cap), the thesis-registration process and the course's rules for using AI in scientific work.

## Summary
The course runs on three Saturdays (26.09., 10.10., 24.10.2026). It covers scientific work with AI, the master-thesis exposé, peer review and
assessing exposés. Its assignment is the first version of the thesis exposé. The slide PDFs themselves are internal teaching material and
are not stored here; only paraphrased notes are.

## Key claims
- **Exposé deadline:** submit via Moodle by **23.10.2026**. A duplicate slide says 23.10.2025, probably an old copy ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Thesis registration:** form plus exposé (Moodle templates) by **31.10.2026**. The programme director approves the topic; a supervisor can be proposed after talking to them first ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Two PDFs:** (1) the exposé, which must **not contain the author's name**; (2) documentation of the tools used for which tasks, plus an estimated AI share in % (0 % = no AI, 100 % = fully AI). **More than 50 % is not allowed** ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Required structure:** Arbeitstitel · Ausgangssituation und Problemstellung · Relevanz · Zielsetzung und Ergebnisse · Vorgehensweise und Methoden · Literatur ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Working title:** simple, self-explanatory to non-experts, about 8–12 words at most, ideally one line, makes the reader curious without revealing results ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Relevance:** both theoretical (knowledge gap) and practical (practical problem) ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Goals:** scientific goals, practical goals, expected results (e.g. a prototype) and the derived research question ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Methods:** theoretical framework, sequence of steps, method per step ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Literature:** prior work, literature on relevance, existing results, references for the planned methods ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Research-question ladder:** Thema → Problem → Problembereich → Forschungsfrage. The question must reflect the state of research and should be fixed with the supervisor ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **AI in scientific work:** levels from "unreflected" (violates good practice) through relief and support to "extension" (AI as a thinking tutor). Document the tools and what they were used for. The lecturer strongly advises against AI for data collection and analysis ([[wa-course-expose]]).
- **Responsibility:** the author must be able to explain and defend every theoretical and methodological decision ([[wa-course-expose]]).
