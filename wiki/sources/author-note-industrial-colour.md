The author's observation that industrial areas have a distinct colour in orthoimagery.

## Summary
In orthoimagery, industrial areas are visibly a different colour from residential and other
land use. Land use is therefore partly recognisable from the image itself, not only from
context.

## Key claims
- The colour of industrial areas differs from other areas in orthoimagery.

## Relevance to this wiki
It corrects the earlier claim in [[cv-detectability]] that land use and building function are
mostly invisible. It also makes `landuse=industrial` a candidate class, and it supports
colour/texture features for a classical ML baseline; see [[spectral-texture-features]].
