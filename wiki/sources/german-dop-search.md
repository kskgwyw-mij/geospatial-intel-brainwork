Search-snippet facts on official German state orthophotos: DOP20/DOP40 nationwide, DOP10 in some Länder, with NRW at 10 cm renewed every 3 years.

## Summary
These facts surfaced in the same search as the Geopera article but come from official sources (AdV, state
open-data catalogues). The raw file also holds the early Geopera snippets, which are superseded by the full
clip ([[geopera-free-satellite-germany]]).

## Key claims
- AdV: digital orthophotos at 20 cm (DOP20) and 40 cm (DOP40) nationwide, and 10 cm in some Länder; metadata on GeoPortal.de.
- NRW: 10 cm, renewed every 3 years. Lower Saxony DOP20: about 3-year actuality. Saxony (Dresden): 20 cm. Mecklenburg-Vorpommern: DOP published as WMTS.
- Commercial comparison: Vexcel urban aerial imagery at 7.5 cm (paid).

## Relevance to this wiki
These are training-imagery options outside Bavaria ([[imagery-sources]]); Bavaria's own DOP20 is covered in [[bayern-dop-regions-search]].
