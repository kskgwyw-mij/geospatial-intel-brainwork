HOK's general study and examination regulation (statute, chapter 1, last amended 8 June 2026); it sets the binding rules for the master thesis, the exposé, the final committee exam and fraud, including unfair AI use.

## Summary
A bilingual statute (German binding, English translation) that applies to all HOK programmes. For the thesis, the
relevant parts are 2.6 (deadlines), 2.8 (deception and fraud), 4.2 (master thesis) and 4.3 (final committee exam).
Unlike the programme regulation [[dsia-studienordnung-2027]], it is the current general version and is not tied to a cohort.

## Key claims
- Version: adopted 12 Dec 2012/2022 (the German and English front pages differ), in force since 1 Mar 2013, last amended by faculty-council resolution on **8 Jun 2026** ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Thesis form (4.2 (1)–(3)):** a scientific, application-oriented, written single-author work from the programme's subject area, oriented to current research questions; it must show the ability to treat a problem scientifically with appropriate method competence ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Length (4.2 (2)):** 10,000–15,000 words, ±10 % allowed; deviations only in justified cases with the programme director's consent ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Exposé (4.2 (4)):** students are encouraged to submit one; the programme director approves it **at the latest during the third semester**. Without an approved exposé, the director assigns a topic ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Supervision (4.2 (5)):** the student may propose a supervisor (who is also first reviewer); the director assigns the supervisor and a second reviewer ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Processing time (4.2 (6)):** starts with topic confirmation and supervisor assignment; **at least 6 months** in total, of which **at least 10 weeks in the fourth semester**. Extensions are possible on request for reasons outside the student's control ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Grading (4.2 (7), (14)):** two written reviews; if either is "Nicht genügend", the thesis is returned for correction (at most twice). The grade is the mean of the two reviews; content/method and formal aspects must each reach ≥ 60 % ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Other thesis rules:** submission per the HOK master-thesis guide (Leitfaden); a sworn declaration of independent work using only the stated sources and aids; a blocking notice (Sperrvermerk) is possible on request; review period at most 6 weeks after the processing time ends ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Final committee exam (4.3):** at least 45 minutes, public, a panel of ≥ 3 people. Weighting: presentation 10 %, **defence of the thesis 50 %**, questions on links to the curriculum and other content 40 %. The defence is meant in particular to validate that the work was done independently. Grades: ≥ 90 % excellent, ≥ 80 % good, ≥ 60 % pass ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Deadline (2.6):** a master thesis must be submitted at the latest 4 semesters after the first possible submission date, or the student leaves the programme ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).
- **Fraud (2.8):** serious fraud, explicitly including submitting a plagiarism or the **unfair use of AI against the standards of good scientific practice**, can lead to termination of the study contract; HOK may revoke the degree for plagiarism up to 10 years after the thesis is graded ([[hok-aspo-2026]]).

## Relevance to this wiki
It fixes the formal frame for [[thesis-timeline]] and [[expose-guide]]: exposé approval by the end of semester 3,
≥ 6 months of work, 10–15k words, and a defence that checks independence. The AI clause matters because this wiki is
LLM-maintained: the author must be able to explain and defend every decision recorded here (see [[hok-ki-leitlinie]]).
