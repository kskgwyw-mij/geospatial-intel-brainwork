The author's observation that road and intersection fingerprints will not work in sparse rural areas.

## Summary
Road networks in the countryside are too sparse to localise an image by roads alone.

## Key claims
- Roads alone will not work in sparse countryside areas.

## Relevance to this wiki
It limits [[costea-2016-aerial-geolocalization]], which was tested only on two cities, and motivates
the rural anchor strategy in [[osm-fingerprint-geolocalization]].
