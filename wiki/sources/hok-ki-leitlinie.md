HOK's guideline on students' use of AI: AI may support learning, structuring and language editing, but must not replace one's own work. Use must be disclosed, and uploading copyrighted teaching or exam material to AI tools is a listed no-go.

## Summary
A 4-page bilingual guideline. Its principle is "support, not substitute". For each area (learning, language, ideas, integrity) it gives allowed uses and
prohibited uses. The PDF is not stored here; only paraphrased notes are.

## Key claims
- **Allowed:** structuring and summarising for one's own learning; explaining complex content; stylistic revision of **self-written** text; spelling, grammar and translation; brainstorming; structuring one's own thoughts ([[hok-ki-leitlinie]]).
- **Prohibited:** submitting AI-generated content as one's own without transparent labelling; fully generating texts where own work is required; adopting AI ideas or arguments without developing them; replacing one's own reflection or analysis ([[hok-ki-leitlinie]]).
- **Prohibited:** entering personal or sensitive data into unapproved AI systems; uploading copyrighted teaching materials or exam documents to AI tools; tools with unclear data processing or without GDPR compliance ([[hok-ki-leitlinie]]).
- **Principle:** when in doubt, AI use is not permitted ([[hok-ki-leitlinie]]).
- **Duties:** follow course requirements, disclose AI use, protect personal data ([[hok-ki-leitlinie]]).

## Relevance to this wiki
This wiki and its tools were built with an AI assistant, so their use has to be documented in the exposé's AI statement
([[wa-course-expose]]) and stay below the 50 % cap. The exposé and thesis prose must be the author's own.
> [!warning] Open point
> The guideline forbids uploading copyrighted teaching materials to AI tools. The WA slide decks and this guideline were uploaded to the
> assistant. Whether HOK counts these as "copyrighted teaching materials", and whether the assistant used here counts as an approved
> system, is for the author to check with the course lecturer (unsourced). The PDFs are therefore not kept in the repository.
