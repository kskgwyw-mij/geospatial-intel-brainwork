Scope decision by the author: the evaluation generalises across **regions only**. There is no cross-provider test, and the ground sampling distance (GSD) is dropped as a generalisation axis.

## Summary
This settles the two open evaluation questions from [[author-note-novelty]] and [[author-decision-title]]. Imagery stays a single provider (Bavarian DOP20). GSD is no longer a study axis; scale is still estimated by the method itself, because the photo has no metadata ([[author-decision-no-metadata]]).

## Key claims
- **No cross-provider test** is planned ([[author-decision-generalisation-axes]]).
- **GSD dropped as a generalisation axis.** It follows the recommendation in [[thesis-contribution]]: a real GSD shift would need multi-altitude drone data, and downsampling is only a proxy ([[author-decision-generalisation-axes]]).
- **Remaining axis: regions**, spatially blocked. Over time, the 2018 vs 2024 flights remain available as an optional check ([[settlement-test]]).
- The subtitle becomes „Eine Generalisierungsstudie über Regionen“ / "A Cross-Region Generalization Study" ([[author-decision-generalisation-axes]]).

## Relevance to this wiki
This narrows the evaluation design and the effort ([[thesis-contribution]], [[thesis-timeline]]). The four-part novelty claim loses "cross-provider", so it must be restated as cross-region on real data.
