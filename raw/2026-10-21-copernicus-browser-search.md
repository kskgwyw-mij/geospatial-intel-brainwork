# Copernicus Browser / Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem (CDSE): web search notes

URL given by the author: https://browser.dataspace.copernicus.eu/
Captured 2026-10-21 from search snippets; the site itself was not reachable from the research environment.

## Browser
- Part of the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem: open, free Earth observation data. A free account is needed (some sources: free to use,
  advanced features unlocked with a free account). https://documentation.dataspace.copernicus.eu/Applications/Browser.html ,
  https://github.com/eu-cdse/copernicus-browser/blob/main/README.md
- Collections grouped as: Copernicus Sentinel missions, Copernicus Contributing Missions (CCM), federated datasets; Sentinel-2 quarterly and annual mosaics.
- Features: search by date, location, source and cloud cover; download full products or single files; pin results for comparison; 3D view.
- CCM systematic collections available in the Browser since 11 Apr 2024. https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/node/1204

## Copernicus DEM
- https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM
- EEA-10: 10 m, 39 European countries (+ French overseas departments); access restricted to a subset of users under the ESA licence.
- GLO-30: 30 m global; GLO-90: 90 m global; free for registered users.
- News (Aug 2026): the GLO-30 *view service* is now limited to authorised CCM user categories; users must opt in to CCM in their profile.
  https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/news/2026-8-25-copernicus-dem-30m-view-service-update

## VHR (Contributing Missions)
- VHR2024 mosaic covers all 39 European states (resolution not stated in the snippets).
  https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/news/2026-4-28-vhr2024-copernicus-contributing-missions-offer-unprecedented-view-europe
- Access depends on user category: Copernicus Services, EU institutions, EU-funded research projects and public authorities can access;
  international organisations and the general public can only view. https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/ccm-user-categories
