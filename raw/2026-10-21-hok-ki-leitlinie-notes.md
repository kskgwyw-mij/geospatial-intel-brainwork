# Notes: HOK "Leitlinie KI & Studium" (AI & study guideline)

Captured 2026-10-21 from the PDF the author uploaded (ki-leitlinie-studium.pdf, 4 pages, German and
English). Treated as internal material: the PDF is **not** stored in this repository. Paraphrased
summary; the two principles marked as quotes are short and quoted because the wording matters.

- AI is a support for learning, not a substitute for acquiring competence. Students remain responsible
  for content, results and compliance; AI use must follow the EU AI Act, GDPR and HOK rules.
- Allowed (examples): structuring learning material, summaries for one's own learning, explaining complex
  content, practice questions; stylistic revision of self-written text, spelling and grammar, translation;
  brainstorming, structuring one's own thoughts, reflecting on solutions and arguments.
- Not allowed (examples): using AI instead of engaging with the material; submitting AI-generated content
  as one's own work; fully generating texts where own work is required; adopting AI ideas or arguments
  without developing them further; AI instead of one's own reflection or analysis; submitting AI content
  without transparent labelling; AI during exams without permission; entering personal or sensitive data
  into unapproved AI systems; **uploading copyrighted teaching materials or exam documents to AI tools**;
  AI systems with unclear data processing or lacking GDPR compliance.
- Principle for the integrity section, quoted: "Im Zweifel ist der Einsatz von KI nicht zulässig."
  (When in doubt, AI use is not permitted.)
- Responsibilities: follow the course requirements, disclose AI use transparently, protect personal data.
- Further guidance is on the HOK intranet.
