# Bavarian orthophotos and southern Upper Bavaria regions: web search notes

Captured 2026-10-21 from web search results (snippets). Verify licence text and URLs before use.

## Bavarian DOP (Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung)
- DOP20 (20 cm ground resolution; colour, grey and colour-infrared) is listed as open data under **CC BY 4.0**.
  Attribution: "Datenquelle: Bayerische Vermessungsverwaltung – www.geodaten.bayern.de". Metadata updated Feb 2026.
  https://twin4clim.gis.lrg.tum.de/dataset/digitales-orthophoto-20-cm-bodenauflosung/resource/9e83b479-60c4-436e-8cb1-a3c45d6206ae
  https://catalog.savenow.gis.lrg.tum.de/dataset/digitales-orthophoto-dop20-wms/resource/0161fd05-5c43-41fd-9057-06fe892ba7f8
- WMS endpoint given in a catalog: https://geoservices.bayern.de/od/wms/dop/v1/dop20? (status not confirmed). Bulk download page not found in the results;
  check geodaten.bayern.de.
- OSM community (May 2026 talk page): the CC BY 4.0 products cannot be used for OSM mapping because of a licence clause
  incompatible with ODbL; DOP80 was switched off as outdated. https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/DE_talk:Luftbilder_aus_Bayern
  https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/DE:Luftbilder_aus_Bayern

## Regions
- No official region named "Oberbayern Süd". Oberbayern planning regions: Ingolstadt, München, Oberland, Südostoberbayern.
- Region 17 Oberland: 3,956 km², population 429,701 (2012); districts Bad Tölz-Wolfratshausen, Garmisch-Partenkirchen, Miesbach, Weilheim-Schongau.
  https://www.region-oberland.bayern.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/RPV17_Sitzungen_140728_VV_TOP3_Aufbau_Organisation_RPV.pdf
- Region 18 Südostoberbayern: 5,225 km², population 792,285 (2012); 159 residents/km² (2017).
  https://www.region-suedostoberbayern.bayern.de/files/RP18_Text_PDF/RP18_Text_A_gesamt.pdf
- Oberbayern total: about 17,529 km².
